05 — Frequently Asked Questions

Technical standards and operational specifications.

What does industrial Maintenance in Operational Condition (MCO) mean?
MCO consists of ensuring the constant availability, regulatory compliance, and physical or logical protection of your technical assets. It incorporates rigorous preventive scheduling and immediate emergency corrective procedures designed to mitigate any threat of factory or infrastructural downtime.
Which types of video protection network servers do you deploy?
As security integration professionals, we build premium server architectures layered with specialized artificial intelligence models. We primarily interface with market-leading hardware and software platforms from Hikvision, Dahua, and CASD for high-end stream parsing in restricted areas.
What are the properties of the JRI My Sirius instrumentation monitoring system?
MCO Facilities is an authorized integrator for the JRI My Sirius ecosystem. This hardware network provides real-time, cloud-connected logging and strict metrological validation of ambient criteria within laboratories, specialized medical storage vaults, and cleanrooms, reporting instantly upon any metric variance.
Are you equipped to manage complex multi-site telemetry arrays?
Yes. Our competencies unify copper wire setups with precise fusion-spliced Fiber Optic (FO) loops, paired with modern Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) packages to map technical monitoring across multiple separated production sites or municipal hubs.
