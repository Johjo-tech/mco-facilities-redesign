05 — Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to your technical requirements.

What is In-Service Support / Operational Readiness (MCO)?
MCO involves ensuring the constant availability, high performance, and strict compliance of technical and industrial installations throughout their operational lifecycle. Our contracts include rigorous preventive maintenance and rapid-response corrective maintenance 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for high-tech infrastructures.
What are your security technologies and certifications?
We operate as an authorized systems integrator for dedicated servers and AI video surveillance solutions (Hik Vision, Dahua, CASD). In addition, MCO Facilities is a certified JRI My Sirius integrator for critical laboratory temperature monitoring and measurement systems.
What geographical area do your teams cover?
Our headquarters are located in Varces (38760) near Grenoble, France. We actively operate throughout the entire Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region (Grenoble, Lyon, Chambéry, Annecy...) and can travel nationwide across France for major accounts and defense infrastructures.
Do you provide a full on-call assistance service?
Yes. Ensuring operational continuity requires constant vigilance. MCO Facilities provides a fully operational technical on-call service 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, specifically dedicated to contracted industrial infrastructures and critical sites.
