Frequently Asked Questions — Video Surveillance

Navigating legal and technical mandates.

Do video surveillance systems require a declaration to the CNIL or regulatory authorities?
For private workspaces not open to the general public, filing an entry in your internal GDPR data processing registry is mandatory, but a direct CNIL declaration is not required. For facilities open to the public, an explicit prefectural authorization must be obtained. Our engineering team handles this entire administrative compliance process on your behalf.
What is the legal retention period for video recordings?
Regulatory codes (such as CNIL directives) restrict image storage to a maximum of 30 days. Retaining data beyond this window requires a verifiable legal anchor (such as an open judicial or police investigation). We configure secure automated 30-day loop purges by default, adjustable to specific local legal limits.
What is the difference between an NVR and a VMS?
An NVR (Network Video Recorder) is a dedicated, closed hardware unit optimized for straightforward plug-and-play local recording. A VMS (Video Management System) is an enterprise software platform capable of orchestrating multi-site links, running deep AI metadata analytics, and offering native API integrations. NVR systems perfectly fit 1-16 camera footprints; beyond that scale or for multi-site business groups, a VMS framework is advised.
Can video surveillance be integrated with access control systems?
Yes, system interlocking is highly recommended. Linking the systems enables instant visual confirmation for every badge swipe event and significantly simplifies historical audit analysis. Both platforms are orchestrated through a single unified operator interface.
What happens to the surveillance system during a network outage?
Our IP camera payloads continue recording locally onto internal SD cards or designated hardware NVR edge storage during data drops, automatically syncing back to the primary server when the link recovers. No footage frames are lost. Automated system health alerts instantly flag operators in the event of a prolonged network issue.
Is AI-driven video analysis fully compliant with GDPR?
Compliance depends on execution. Running object sorting, directional crossing, or anomalous behavior heuristics is fully legal and compliant. Conversely, live biometric facial recognition mapping requires specialized, high-tier government or pre-authorized clearances. We help steer your design choices to ensure you stay within the bounds of permitted legal applications.
