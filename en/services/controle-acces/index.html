Frequently Asked Questions — Video Surveillance

Everything you need to know about legal compliance.

Do video surveillance systems need to be declared to the Data Protection Authority?
For private workspaces not open to the public, registration in your internal GDPR data processing log is mandatory, but no direct declaration to the Data Protection Authority is required. For locations open to the public, a prefectural authorization is necessary. We manage the entire compliance process for you.
What is the legal retention period for video footage?
Data protection rules recommend a maximum of 30 days. Beyond this, any extended timeframe must be officially justified by a legitimate need (such as ongoing legal proceedings or police investigations). We configure a 30-day automatic overwrite cycle by default, which can be adjusted to match your declared setup.
What is the difference between an NVR and a VMS?
An NVR (Network Video Recorder) is a dedicated, closed hardware appliance designed for simple recording. A VMS (Video Management System) is an open software enterprise framework capable of managing multi-site systems, integrating smart video analytics, access systems, and advanced AI. An NVR fits setups with 1 to 16 cameras perfectly; for larger deployment or multi-site setups, a VMS is highly recommended.
Can video surveillance be coupled with access control?
Yes, it is highly recommended. Integrating both systems allows for visual validation during each badge check and easy retrospective audits of entries and exits. Both functions are handled through a single, unified interface.
What happens in the event of a network outage?
IP cameras record locally onto edge-storage micro SD cards or directly to the local NVR during downtime. They seamlessly sync back up with the cloud once infrastructure comes online. No footage is lost. Critical alerts are triggered if an outage persists.
Is AI-driven video analysis GDPR compliant?
It depends entirely on the usage. Object and non-biometric anomaly detection is compliant. Conversely, live facial recognition requires strict regulatory clearance from relevant data privacy authorities. We will guide you toward permitted configurations based on your operational context.
