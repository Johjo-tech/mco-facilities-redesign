Frequently Asked Questions — Automation & Robotics

Technical insights on automated production.

Schneider Electric or Siemens — which PLC should I choose?
Both ranges are excellent. Schneider Electric (Modicon M340/M580, EcoStruxure) leads in France and the food industry; Siemens (S7-1200/1500, TIA Portal) dominates exported lines and precision mechatronics. The real criterion is your existing installed base and the skills of your maintenance teams. We program and maintain both, with no lock-in to a single brand.
What is a SCADA and what is it for?
A SCADA (Supervisory Control And Data Acquisition) is the supervision station that collects real-time data from PLCs, displays line status, logs alarms, and drives production. It gives operators a synoptic view of the site and enables downtime analysis, traceability, and decision support. We deploy Wonderware/AVEVA, WinCC, PcVue, and Ignition.
Can a line be modernized without replacing everything?
Yes — that is the whole point of a retrofit. We keep the sound mechanical part and replace only the obsolete control hardware: end-of-support PLC, drives, HMI, wiring. You recover a high-performing, fully documented machine for a fraction of the cost of new equipment, with controlled production downtime.
Are variable speed drives worth the investment?
On pumps and fans, a variable frequency drive typically cuts energy consumption by 20 to 50% by matching speed to actual demand instead of running at full power with mechanical throttling. Payback is often between 1 and 3 years, and mechanical wear drops sharply thanks to soft starts.
What is an industrial network like Profinet or OPC-UA?
These are the buses that let PLCs, drives, sensors, and supervision communicate. Profinet and EtherCAT handle real-time control on the shop floor, Modbus TCP links third-party equipment, and OPC-UA feeds data up to supervision and enterprise IT (MES/ERP) in a secure, standardized way. A clean network architecture is the foundation of a reliable, cyber-secure site.
Do you provide maintenance after commissioning?
Yes. We provide maintenance and support for the installations we integrate, including remote diagnostics, on-call service, and program backups. This is also at the heart of our Operational Readiness (MCO) offering, which covers 24/7 availability of your critical equipment.
