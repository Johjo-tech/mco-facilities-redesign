Our MCO Framework

Six strategic vectors, one unified service contract.

Preventive Maintenance

Tailored engineering plans based on original manufacturer parameters, statutory code compliance, and localized facility wear history. Monthly, quarterly, or bi-annual technical sweeps customized per asset class.

Corrective Intervention

Contractual SLA commitments for both Response Time (GTI) and Resolution Time (GTR). Parametric tracking workflows split by priority class: P1 Critical Failures, P2 Major Anomalies, and P3 Minor Fixes.

Shared Cloud CMMS

Every field work order, regulatory certificate, equipment manual, layout diagram, and site photo is centralized into a transparent asset management portal available 24/7 to you, your operators, and financial auditors.

Monthly Analytics & Reporting

Automated metrics dashboards documenting technical uptime percentages, Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF), Mean Time to Repair (MTTR), ticket volume distribution by engineering trade, and system performance advice.

24/7 On-Call Support

A single dedicated phone number with incoming calls answered in under 5 minutes by qualified dispatchers. Remote diagnostic validation followed by immediate on-site deployment in line with your contract SLAs.

CAPEX Strategy & Lifecycle Planning

Strategic consultation regarding infrastructure lifecycle limits, technical obsolescence warnings, and efficiency upgrades. We help your management balance multi-year OPEX vs CAPEX trades.

Frequently Asked Questions — MCO

What technical directors always verify.

What is the difference between preventive and corrective maintenance?
Preventive maintenance comprises scheduled technical tasks built into a timeline to stop components from breaking down (oil changes, calibration, visual sweeps, wear-part tracking). Corrective maintenance deploys field technicians immediately post-fault to recover standard operation under clear SLA paths. A well-designed MCO strategy balances both — the former directly minimizes the latter.
What are P1, P2, and P3 maintenance contracts?
Commonly used across European enterprise and public facility management, P1 governs utility fuel and power supply costs, P2 handles routine preventive care and minor component fixes, and P3 covers major mechanical overhauls and complete equipment replacement insurance. MCO Facilities handles all three tiers, adapted to your asset portfolio.
How does operational reporting work?
Automated reporting sheets are delivered via PDF alongside 24/7 web-portal dashboard access. Monitored indices include technical uptime rates, MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures), MTTR (Mean Time to Repair), ticket distributions classified by trade type, unusual energy spikes, and long-term optimization recommendations.
What is your average emergency on-call response time?
For properties active under our supervision contracts, our baseline response commitment is 2 hours during normal business shifts and 4 hours during night or weekend hours for business-critical faults. Express SLAs (30 mins to 1 hour) can be implemented based on site criticality. First-line emergency phone calls are consistently handled in under 5 minutes.
Which sectors do you support with MCO?
We manage commercial workspaces, heavy industrial lines, logistics properties, healthcare assets, commercial retail chains, luxury hospitality spaces, housing associations, and regional authority sites. Our technical engineering approach addresses the specific operational drivers of each sector, whether prioritizing tenant comfort, zero production downtime, or transparent compliance auditing.
