Service 02 — MCO

Operational readiness for your automation and security systems

A frozen supervision screen, a faulty PLC, cameras that stop reporting: on an industrial, hospital or military site, every failure has a cost. MCO Facilities provides preventive and corrective maintenance for your technical installations, with 24/7 on-call support and a contact who knows your site.

Our scope

What ourMCO covers.

Automation and supervision

Schneider Electric and other PLC brands, SCADA supervision (PCvue), program backup and updates, retrofit of obsolete PLCs.

Video protection

Hikvision and Dahua cameras and recorders, dedicated servers, video supervision platforms (CASD), stream-loss monitoring.

Intrusion alarms

Honeywell Galaxy and Vanderbilt control panels: troubleshooting, programming, extensions.

Temperature monitoring

Certified JRI My Sirius integrator for laboratories and regulated enclosures.

Networks and power

Copper and fibre optic networks, equipment power supply, testing and acceptance.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently askedquestions.

Do you work on installations you did not install?
Yes, that is the most common case. We start with an audit to take over the documentation and back up the existing programs.
Can you take over a PLC or supervision system with no documentation?
Yes. We retrieve the programs in place, back them up and rebuild the documentation needed.
Does the on-call service cover nights and weekends?
Yes, 24/7 for sites under contract.
Do you bid for public contracts?
Yes, we respond to tenders from local authorities and public bodies, with a qualified electronic signature.
