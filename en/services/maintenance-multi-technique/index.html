Why MCO Matters

Maintenance is not an expense, it is an insurance policy.

01

A critical HVAC failure during peak summer at a logistics hub can mean up to €30,000 in ruined inventory per day. Proactive MCO anticipates system vulnerabilities before they ever hit operations.

Our MCO Core Capability

Six strategic pillars, one unified master agreement.

Preventive Maintenance

Tailor-made maintenance schedules engineered around manufacturer guidelines, legal compliance, and historic site usage data. Monthly, quarterly, or bi-annual site visits based on system profiles.

Corrective Maintenance

Contractual commitments on response times (GTI) and resolution deadlines (GTR). Configurable urgency routing across standardized priority levels (P1 critical, P2 major, P3 minor).

Shared CMMS Tool

Every single service slip, maintenance contract, statutory compliance check, site plan, and photo is centralized in an intuitive cloud platform accessible 24/7 by your team, operators, and compliance officers.

Monthly Reporting

Automated dashboards summarizing infrastructure status: overall system availability, MTBF, MTTR, ticket distribution by technology category, energy usage analytics, and engineering recommendations.

24/7 On-Call Support

A single dedicated phone number, certified engineers on stand-by, and inbound call processing under 5 minutes. Initial remote verification followed by rapid on-site dispatch matching your contractual SLAs.

CAPEX Life-Cycle Steering

Strategic advising on machinery lifespans, end-of-life planning, and energy efficiency upgrades. We partner with you to weigh and optimize the long-term OPEX/CAPEX balance over a 5 to 10-year window.

MCO defined in one sentence: Ensuring that a technical installation functions, performs, and stays fully compliant throughout its entire operational lifecycle, by seamlessly blending scheduled maintenance, swift repairs, and data-driven management indicators.

Frequently Asked Questions — MCO

Common questions asked by facility and operations managers.

What is the difference between preventive and corrective maintenance?
Preventive maintenance comprises scheduled visits aimed at preserving hardware health and avoiding unexpected faults (fluid flushes, component checks, wear-and-tear swap-outs). Corrective maintenance covers reactive fixing immediately following an incident to get the equipment back online. A robust MCO plan integrates both to drastically lower overall failure rates.
What is a P1, P2, P3 framework contract?
Derived from traditional public utility and asset procurement, a P1 tier manages raw fuel, utilities, and heating supplies; P2 dictates standard routine upkeep and preventative tasks; P3 handles heavy overhauls, mechanical renewals, and asset substitution. MCO Facilities provides all three parameters, completely modular to fit your operational criteria.
How does the performance reporting work?
You receive an automated monthly PDF report alongside 24/7 access to your online client dashboard. Tracked analytics include: overall system uptime rates, MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures), MTTR (Mean Time To Repair), breakdown distribution metrics, total energy profiles, and continuous optimization feedback.
What is your average response time for emergency on-call requests?
For assets wrapped in our supervision contract, our standard commitment for critical structural errors is 2 hours inside business windows and 4 hours during night or weekend periods. Tailored, tighter SLAs (such as 30 minutes or 1 hour response matrices) can be built for key business infrastructure. Inbound emergency dispatching is always acknowledged within 5 minutes.
Which sectors do you support with Operational Readiness services?
We manage commercial workspaces, factories, logistics platforms, healthcare infrastructures (nursing locations, specialty clinics), retail networks, hotels, and public/municipal properties. Our field execution adapts immediately to match your specific asset risks — focusing on regulatory compliance, continuity of production, occupant comfort, or budget transparency.
