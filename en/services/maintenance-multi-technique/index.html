System Coverage

Five core building trades, one unified team.

HVAC — Heating, Ventilation & Air Conditioning

Splits, multi-splits, VRV/VRF systems, air handling units (AHU), single and double-flow mechanical ventilation (MVHR), gas/oil boilers, and air-to-water or water-to-water heat pumps. Full F-Gas Category I certification.

Electrical Engineering

Main low-voltage switchboards (MLVS), distribution panelboards, emergency exit lighting systems, surge protection devices, annual thermal imaging inspections, and regulatory compliance checks (Q18/Q19 checks).

Plumbing & Sanitary Systems

Domestic cold and hot water infrastructure, system flushing and sludge removal, legionella prevention management (sanitary logs and sample testing), accessible PMR sanitary units, and automated shut-off valves.

Low-Voltage & Safety Systems

Category A to E fire detection systems, APSAD R81 certified intrusion alarms, technical monitoring sensors (server room environments, water level indicators), intercom setups, and public address arrays.

BMS / Building Automation

Technical facility supervision via open architecture structures. Multi-protocol integration (BACnet, Modbus TCP/RTU, KNX, M-Bus). Energy usage reporting, context-sensitive alerts, and full remote control logic.

Regulatory Auditing & Compliance

Coordinating with independent inspection bodies (Apave, Bureau Veritas, Socotec), monitoring Q18/Q19 clearances, certificate management, and tracking non-compliance resolutions. We manage the timeline, you sign off.

Our Core Principle: A building operates as a unified ecosystem, not an isolated collection of trades. A plumbing leak can take down an MLVS switchboard; an AHU breakdown can trigger a server room overheat. We look at the comprehensive picture and act where it matters most.

Frequently Asked Questions — Multi-technical

What technical directors always verify.

What does 'multi-technical' maintenance cover?
It bridges every core technical system of an infrastructure: HVAC (heating, ventilation, cooling loops), electrical (MLVS panels, sub-boards, safety lighting), plumbing (cold/hot networks, anti-legionella logs, descaling), low voltage (fire alarms, intrusion detection, tech alerts), and BMS (automated supervision layers).
Do you use in-house technicians or sub-contractors?
Our operations team is 100% in-house and fully certified (low/medium voltage clearance, F-Gas handlers, rope/high-altitude work). For ultra-specialized scopes (elevators, deep asbestos abatement, heavy structural scaling), we coordinate with vetted partner networks under fully transparent service terms.
Can you take over the maintenance of an existing building?
Yes, this is very common. We begin operations with a comprehensive site technical audit of the physical inventory, logging asset degradation vectors and missing regulatory testing certificates, then transition operations without system downtime using a remediation schedule if needed.
Do you work on open BMS architectures?
Yes. We work entirely within standardized communication backbones like BACnet, Modbus TCP/RTU, KNX, and M-Bus. We do not use closed proprietary software setups, giving you total freedom to transition systems without re-engineering hardware lines down the road.
Are your teams certified to handle refrigerants?
Yes, our engineering staff possess Category I capacity certifications (permitting operations across all tonnage classes) matching regional F-Gas frameworks. Leak test logging registers and annual consumption reports are handled natively under the contract framework.
How does the handover transition from my current vendor work?
We plan a structured 1 to 3-month transition phase depending on system scale. Our engineers recover master as-built blueprints, third-party vendor arrangements, and active CMMS history logs, systematically absorbing technical oversight over the assets. Zero operational interruption.
